# Adaptive EDA for master-data candidate discovery

**Repository revision:** `7c0dfc9b57ce8c2aa7a343d6b7ebd6b1a4a46313` (`origin/main`, 2026-11-21).

## Finding

**Recommendation:** integrate EDA as a focused, evidence-led extension of the existing Data Profiling run—not as a separate data-science cleaning or visualization phase. The profiler already measures several useful candidate signals: key uniqueness, links between parts, repeated identifier-plus-name evidence for entities embedded in rows, measured classification tests, and some temporal roles. These can identify *parts worth reviewing as entity candidates*; they cannot settle business meaning, MDM kind, identity namespace, or operator approval. The findings contract requires evidence for claims and says a class the tests do not choose is `silver`, not a guessed class. [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L10-L26]

## What the repository measures

| Evidence | Recorded behavior and relevance to candidate discovery |
|---|---|
| **Key/reuse and structural role** | Column profiles include row/non-null/distinct counts, uniqueness, type, top values, and shape/length information. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/profile.py:L51-L98] The profiler selects a key from unique keys (or a designed key when none is found), and records incoming links as one classification input. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/keys.py:L255-L288] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/classify.py:L22-L30] Uniqueness alone is not semantic identity: the modeling guidance requires a new kind to have a persistent identifier or durable designed key, references from other records or another source, and no existing kind match—all three. [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L171-L185]
| **Relationships / references** | A link is measured from a column to a key in another part using inclusion, coverage, randomness, and name similarity; accepted links record cardinality and whether the target was fully read or sampled. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/keys.py:L126-L147] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/keys.py:L150-L196] Classification uses link directions and link-key evidence to distinguish master, relationship, and transaction candidates. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/classify.py:L22-L59] This is structural evidence, not proof of the relationship's business meaning. Modeling policy says permitted endpoint pairs or metadata lineage do not confirm actual relationships. [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L48-L52] [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L64-L72]
| **Embedded/repeated entities** | The implementation can propose a separate part when an identifier-shaped value repeats across at least 20 identifiers and a name-like field follows it on at least 95% of repeated rows; it excludes stand-ins and identifiers that look like links to an existing part. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/embedded.py:L1-L27] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/embedded.py:L96-L122] The resulting part is still classified by the same tests, and disagreements in carried values are counted for quality review. [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L178-L197] This is a measured candidate heuristic, not authoritative entity semantics.
| **Class test evidence** | Master tests require a unique key and also score inbound references, few outgoing links, name-like text, relative row count, and attributes beyond codes/dates. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/classify.py:L22-L30] The selected class is the unique top class with more than half its tests passing; ties or weaker results become `silver`, and the tests, confidence, and runner-up are returned. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/classify.py:L70-L84] Treat a `master` result as a candidate supported by those tests, not a final domain/kind decision.
| **Temporal evidence** | The profiler records candidate valid-time pairs when named start/end dates are ordered in at least 99% of rows, names a record-time column from generic name words, and assigns an event-time candidate to remaining temporal columns (excluding personal dates). It also measures versions-per-key in a limited key/time case and regular time-series steps/gaps. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/timing.py:L1-L5] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/timing.py:L24-L51] These signals can describe temporal behavior; the schema leaves delivery and refresh unknown until `compare` sees another delivery. [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L104-L111] The schema describes cross-delivery persistence/change/addition evidence as a `compare` result, and explicitly keeps persistence/delivery/refresh unknown until a second delivery. [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L148-L172] Therefore one profile cannot establish lifecycle persistence or growth across deliveries.
| **Identifier and pairing evidence** | Identifier-shaped columns are detected by shape/length/pattern rules; identifier check-digit evidence is computed over distinct values with pass rates and chance rates. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/identifiers.py:L153-L189] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/identifiers.py:L192-L220] For master parts, `pairs` counts one-to-one, one-to-many, missing, and containment relations among identifiers without recording the actual paired values. [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L134-L147] Such evidence can support a human identity review, but the modeling skill says matching identifiers must be declared by the kind's Identifier Contract; all other joins are disallowed. [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L142-L178]

## Gaps and boundaries

- **No final semantic MDM-kind determination:** `_part` starts `proposed_kind` as null, but `profile_inputs` later calls `_propose_kinds`; for a top-level `master` class without a matched kind, it drafts a kind from the part name and passed class-test names, then `_questions` recommends approval. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/run.py:L178-L196] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/run.py:L590-L605] This draft does not prove all three Data Modeling tests, especially persistence across deliveries. The final kind remains Data Modeling's and the operator's decision. [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L50-L56] [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L171-L185]
- **No complete lifecycle proof from one delivery:** time-role inference is based on names/value consistency, and delivery/refresh stay unknown pending another delivery. [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/timing.py:L24-L51] [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L148-L172]
- **No resolution of meaning or matching authority:** identifier shape, uniqueness, pairing, and link inclusion are measured evidence, not permission to merge records or define a namespace. The modeling policy limits matching to declared Identifier Contract namespaces and says a new namespace changes merging and needs an operator ruling. [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L142-L178]
- **Source-policy conflict with generic EDA advice:** the Data Scientist skill recommends previewing rows, always plotting distributions/relationships, and cleaning by reshaping, removing duplicates, adding identifiers, imputing or dropping values. [skills/data-scientist/SKILL.md:L48-L75] Data Profiling instead says use local copies only, never guess a class/key/link, mask samples, do not record raw personal values, and never approve findings on the operator's behalf. [skills/data-profiling/SKILL.md:L92-L103] For this skill integration, do not import row previews, mandatory charts, data mutation, duplicate removal, imputation, or generated IDs as profiling behavior. Prefer measured counts and masked summaries; a data scientist may explore separately only under the appropriate data-use and privacy controls.

## Recommended lean integration

1. **Start with the existing findings and sample evidence.** Reuse the profile's field counts/shapes and tests before widening. Existing adaptive profiling says a field with one shape across filled rows is done; widen a field only when the sample finds multiple shapes, an unparseable shape, or rows fitting none, and stop that field when an additional pass adds no shape. Limit work to fields feeding a silver field, key, link, or matching rule. [skills/data-profiling/SKILL.md:L88-L100]
2. **Widen only candidate evidence that can change a modeling handoff.** As a recommendation, focus follow-up reads on candidate key/identifier uniqueness and reuse, links to candidate parts, embedded-entity follow rates/clashes, and time-role/persistence evidence. Preserve the profiler's full-versus-sampled indication; the existing report schema distinguishes the scan mode and carries it with evidence. [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L37-L46] [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L124-L147] Do not add broad visualization or scans of unrelated descriptive columns.
3. **Stop at convergence or an explicit evidence gap.** As a recommendation, stop candidate-focused widening when a targeted full verification adds no new identity/reuse, relationship, or temporal evidence and no candidate's measured class changes. If a required signal is absent, mark the candidate unresolved and route a profiler-measurement gap to a ticket for a later source; the profiling policy says an open question goes to onboarding's one test/Proving Run, while a missing measurement needs its own profiler fix, and approved findings must not be rescanned just to fill the gap. [skills/data-profiling/SKILL.md:L199-L210] For existing shape profiling, retain its explicit one-more-pass/no-new-shape stop rule. [skills/data-profiling/SKILL.md:L91-L100]
4. **Report candidates, not verdicts.** Summarize candidate part, measured class/tests and confidence, found-versus-designed key, identifier shapes/pair counts, links and their scan/evidence, embedded follow/clash counts, temporal observations, and any `proposed_kind` draft. The schema has those evidence fields; `run` can draft a kind from class tests but does not verify cross-delivery persistence. [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L61-L110] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/run.py:L590-L605] Explain which facts support identity/reuse, which remain semantic questions, and what evidence is missing; distinguish a structural relationship from a business relationship. [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L48-L56]
5. **Keep privacy and approval gates intact.** Never introduce raw values into the EDA summary: profiling policy explicitly says report counts rather than values and masks samples; the schema also excludes key hashes from the approval file and keeps them in a separate fingerprint file. [skills/data-profiling/SKILL.md:L101-L105] [docs/specs/profiling/findings.md:L15-L25] [skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/report.py:L21-L35] Require operator review/approval of findings in exact words, then pass approved evidence to Data Modeling for kind, identifier, and relationship decisions; Data Modeling makes the class/destination decision and hands the approved mapping onward. [skills/data-profiling/SKILL.md:L92-L103] [skills/data-modeling/SKILL.md:L50-L56]

**Bottom line:** existing measures are sufficient to nominate and prioritize candidate entity parts, especially through key/reuse, relationship, embedded-entity, and time evidence. They do not establish semantic identity, MDM kind, or lifecycle persistence from one delivery. Keep the integration generic and read-only; widen narrowly, stop on evidence convergence, and leave semantic classification and approval to Data Modeling and the operator.
## Current and retained-source tests

An operator-authorized, local-only profile of the current GLEIF Level 1 and Relationships files scanned 3,502,540 and 493,650 rows, respectively. The full scan produced five parts and six links, with no proposed kind or generated question. The leading Level 1 part tied `master` and `transaction` at five of six tests and remained `silver`; the Relationships part classified as `relationship` at four of four tests. The embedded successor part tied `master` and `transaction` at four of six tests and had 0% name-follow evidence, so it remains unresolved. No approval, comparison, or store write followed. The class-test behavior is implemented in [the classifier](../../skills/data-profiling/scripts/profiling/classify.py#L70-L84); the GLEIF source declares its inputs and mastering target in [its source contract](../../sources/gleif/source.yaml#L13-L69).

This run supports the visual-review rule to include `silver` parts whose master tests tie for the top score, while preserving `class: silver`. Links and an embedded part alone did not establish a master entity.

Data Operations located the SEC Company captures under `~/.local/share/edgartools/mdm/captures/sec.submissions.company/`; the operator accepted the older local snapshot. The `cm05-7000` profile used 7,000 submission documents and its 2026-09-02 ticker catalog. It yielded eight parts, including 3,881,216 recent-filing rows, 10,391 catalog rows and 7,000 company rows. The Company part was identified by CIK but remained `silver` (0.0), with no proposed kind. A derived filing-identifier part had 3,823,625 rows, classified `master` (1.0), and produced one provisional kind proposal and one generated question; the filing rows themselves were `transaction` (1.0). A ticker-to-catalog link was measured, but no shared Company master domain was established. The derived class is structural evidence, not a domain verdict; transaction grain and cross-source use remain decisive. No approval, comparison or store write followed.

NER (named-entity recognition) detects typed text spans; entity resolution links mentions to canonical identities under approved Identifier Contracts and matching rules. The SEC inputs here were submissions JSON and a ticker catalog, not filing-body text, so this cohort did not test NER. An NER evaluation needs representative adjudicated span labels and held-out precision/recall by entity type; MDM identity resolution separately needs persistent identifiers, cross-source evidence, and a governed path for ambiguous matches.
