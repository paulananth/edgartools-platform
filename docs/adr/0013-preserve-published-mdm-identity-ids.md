# Preserve published MDM identity IDs through replay

Accepted on 2026-09-18 in the [Clean MDM policy interview](../../.scratch/clean-mdm/issues/01-set-merge-stage-policy.md), Q3: keep an internal identity ID independent of source identifiers and role changes, retain losing IDs as aliases after an approved consolidation, and restore the saved identity registry and accepted decisions for exact-ID replay. This makes registry/history retention part of the recovery contract; a source-only rebuild cannot promise identical published IDs. The survivor-selection rule remains an open decision.
