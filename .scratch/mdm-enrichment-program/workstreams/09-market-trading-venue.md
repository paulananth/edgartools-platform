# Market and trading-venue enrichment

Classification: mandatory consumer
Status: planned
Depends on: shared foundation; MIC mapping; explicit Market/Venue domain
Future spec: `docs/specs/mdm-enrichment/market-venue.md`

## Destination

Represent accepted MIC-to-LEI evidence as a Market or Trading Venue operated by
a legal entity. MIC must not be stored as a Company identifier.

The spec must define operating-organization identity, venue hierarchy, code
status and history, conflicts, and links to Security market data without mixing
dated market facts into legal-entity identity.
