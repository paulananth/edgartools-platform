# Sole-proprietor and Person boundary

Classification: mandatory decision before consumer
Status: planned
Depends on: shared foundation; privacy and identity review
Future spec: `docs/specs/mdm-enrichment/sole-proprietor.md`

## Destination

Define how `SOLE_PROPRIETOR` evidence represents a registered business capacity
without treating an LEI as authority for unrelated natural-person identity.

The spec must settle Person-versus-business identifiers, privacy classification,
permitted attributes and relationships, redaction, access, retention, and the
conditions under which evidence remains captured but unpublished.
