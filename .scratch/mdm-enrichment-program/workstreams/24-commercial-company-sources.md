# Other commercial-company sources

Classification: conditional source family
Status: research required per source
Depends on: shared foundation; Company authority contract
Future spec: `docs/specs/mdm-enrichment/conditional/commercial-company.md`

Provide a governed entry point for additional company registries and commercial
profiles without creating a blanket vendor approval. Every proposed source owns
an individual `adopt`, trigger-bound `defer`, or `reject` decision and must prove
authority, licensing, incremental lift over SEC/GLEIF, identity safety, cadence,
cost, replay, retention, and domain ownership.
