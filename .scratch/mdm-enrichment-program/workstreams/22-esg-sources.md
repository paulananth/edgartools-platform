# ESG sources

Classification: conditional source family
Status: research required
Depends on: shared foundation
Future spec: `docs/specs/mdm-enrichment/conditional/esg.md`

Evaluate each ESG publisher's methodology, license, coverage, restatement
behavior, entity/security scope, point-in-time history, measurable user value,
cost, and reproducibility. Scores from different methodologies remain source-
grained and must not be collapsed into one authoritative Company fact without a
separate model decision.
