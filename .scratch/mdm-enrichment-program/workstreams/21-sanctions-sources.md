# Sanctions sources

Classification: conditional source family
Status: research required
Depends on: shared foundation; legal/privacy review
Future spec: `docs/specs/mdm-enrichment/conditional/sanctions.md`

Evaluate official sanctions publishers separately by jurisdiction. The source
must remain authority for its own list and dates; fuzzy identity matches cannot
become definitive sanctions assertions. The source decision must specify false-
positive review, effective-time history, removal semantics, access controls,
replay, and legal/operational ownership.
