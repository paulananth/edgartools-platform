# Set retirement, release, and operating authority

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03

## Question

What evidence permits business retirement or physical deletion, what gates a
consumer release, and which actors may capture, review, release, or delete?

## Answer

Business-close only from an explicit source change or complete reconciliation;
preserve history and require successor revalidation. Preserve normalized
evidence, manifests, hashes, lineage, and decisions; raw archives may transition
or expire only through reviewed exact-version retention after all dependencies
clear. Every consumer must prove complete inventory, terminal dispositions,
identity uniqueness, source-grained provenance, semantic correctness,
deterministic replay, fail-closed checkpoints, downstream parity, rollback, and
approved cost. Capture worker, domain publisher, steward, Release Owner, and
retention operator have separate authority. Future heuristic auto-linking needs
an independent 99.9% lower confidence bound and zero uniqueness violations.
