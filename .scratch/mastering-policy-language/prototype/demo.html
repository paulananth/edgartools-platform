Mastering Policy — prototype

The question: can de-duplication and merge rules live in a configuration document instead of in code — and can an operator read that document and predict what it will do?

Below, a reporting owner from a Form 3/4/5 filing is classified by rule C-J, read out of policy-person.json. Nothing here is hard-coded: the steps, their order, the 125 legal-form words, the six SEC profile fields and the 99% bar all come from that file. Change the name or a field below and watch which step fires.

A verdict is automatic only when the document carries a proof for that exact rule version and that exact verdict. C-J has one for person and none for entity — which is why the same rule runs alone for one answer and goes to a Steward for the other.
