## Decisions so far

- [01 — Survey financial-services ER skill requirements per new Explore product](issues/01-survey-er-skill-requirements-per-product.md) — earnings-analysis is the hardest gate on all four products (mandatory dated+hyperlinked source citations, exact-quarter transcript match ±1 day, guide-vs-guide history); GUIDANCE_FACTS' checklist must treat "no guidance issued" as a valid explicit outcome, not a row-count failure; TRANSCRIPT_EVENTS' `PILOT_CIKS={320193}`+latest-only misses earnings-preview (needs *prior*-quarter call) and initiating-coverage (needs 2-3 quarters) on history depth independent of CIK breadth; idea-generation/sector-overview have zero textual basis for their matrix `Gap` cells on Consensus.
- [03 — Product-ready promotion criteria for CONSENSUS_ESTIMATES](issues/03-promotion-criteria-consensus-estimates.md) — 8-criterion checklist (50% universe coverage, trailing-4Q pre-earnings freshness, source scope restricted to `{yahoo, firm_manual}`, format-verified yahoo `source_ref` authenticity, git-reviewed-artifact provenance for firm_manual, grain coherence, identity-checked join, Explore-only labeling). Load-bearing finding: no fabrication check based on a banned-string-prefix (e.g. `demo:*`) would have caught tonight's own incident — the real fix is source-scope restriction + yahoo's deterministic `source_ref` format, not string pattern-matching.
- [02 — Reclassify the 4 stale Gap rows](issues/02-reclassify-stale-gap-rows.md) — 13 cells mechanically Gap→Partial (real need + real product now exists); 3 corrections: Consensus+as-of's idea-generation/sector-overview Gap→N/A (zero textual basis), Transcript's morning-note Gap→N/A (scheduling-only need, covered by a different row), Transcript's initiating-coverage N/A→Partial (matrix previously missed a real 2-3-quarter need). No cell reached Covered — none of the 4 products pass their own promotion checklist yet. Applied directly to `.scratch/er-data-plane/coverage-matrix.md` (new footnotes F16–F19).
- [04 — Product-ready promotion criteria for GUIDANCE_FACTS](issues/04-promotion-criteria-guidance-facts.md) — Apple's `rows_earnings_release: 0` reframed: it's a more basic parser-matching signal, not proof 8-K is unreliable for guidance specifically. Real finding: `has_guidance` and the extractor both silently swallow *any* exception as "no guidance" — confirmed as a **blocking prerequisite** (fix before promotion is evaluated at all), not an accepted risk. 10-criterion checklist incl. 90% extraction-yield bar (replaces the unqualified "is 8-K reliable" question with a measured threshold), current+prior-quarter guide-vs-guide, format-verified `sec_8k:` `source_ref` authenticity (`guidance_facts.py:557`).
- [06 — Product-ready promotion criteria for TRANSCRIPT_EVENTS](issues/06-promotion-criteria-transcript-events.md) — Covered status **differs by consuming skill**: earnings-analysis (latest-quarter-only need) is structurally satisfiable Apple-only, gated by 6 criteria incl. re-deriving `derive_ir_event_id` from a row's own fields as a free authenticity proof and requiring `store_transcript_text`-sourced content (not a bare `register_ir_pointer`, which satisfies citation but not earnings-analysis's quotable-content need). earnings-preview (needs *prior* quarter) and initiating-coverage (needs 2-3 quarters) stay Partial regardless of pilot quality — recorded as a blocking multi-quarter-history build dependency, not a checkable criterion.
