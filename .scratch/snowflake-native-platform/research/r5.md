# R5: Ontology (Snowflake business ontology against AutoOntology)

## Question

What is Snowflake's business ontology (and semantic views, Cortex Analyst's use of them)? Does it hold what AutoOntology holds (terms, typed relations such as issued by and held by, mappings from terms to columns)? Is it GA, preview or announced?

## Status

- **Business Ontology: private preview, selected accounts only.** From the documentation: "Support for this feature is currently not in production and is available only to selected accounts." Not in the public docs navigation. No region or edition limit is stated. Confidence high.
- **Announced, not verified:** a search snippet says a public preview is "coming". I did not confirm this on a Snowflake page. Treat as announced only.
- **Semantic views, define: GA** (from the documentation, release notes). Snowsight create and manage: GA 2 Oct 2025. Semantic View Autopilot: GA per the Feb 2026 release, from the documentation. Confidence high.
- **Semantic views, query: preview** at last check (summit-era notes). I did not find a newer page. Confidence medium.
- **Many-to-many relationships and `using_relationships`: "Preview Feature - Open", available to all accounts** (from the documentation, YAML spec page).

## Comparison

| Capability | Ours (AutoOntology and catalog) | Snowflake | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terms and definitions | Owned in `CONTEXT.md` and rules; projected to OpenMetadata glossary. AutoOntology proposes concepts, kept as proposals until reviewed | Business Ontology Term nodes with definition, owner, approval status, synonyms, domain (from the documentation, private preview) | Covered in design. Ours has no in-product approval state; Snowflake's is draft to approved to canonical |
| Typed relations (issued by, held by) | Relationship types come from the RDM authority in data-modeling. AutoOntology proposes joins and relations with direction kept | Node relationships such as "has part" and "applies to", plus "derives" edges. The documentation does not mention "issued by" or "held by" or any typed vocabulary. Semantic-view relationships are join-only (no typed field beyond `asof` and `range`) | Partial. Snowflake has links, not a governed type vocabulary. We would keep our RDM types as the vocabulary |
| Mapping terms to columns | AutoOntology maps tables and columns; concepts are reviewed against repo definitions | Representations bind a node to a table, view, column, or a part of a semantic view. Semantic-view `expr` accepts any SQL expression (from the documentation) | Covered for binding. Our mapping to exact tables comes from the exposure list; Snowflake's does not state a table scope rule |
| Column profiling, sampled values, embeddings | AutoOntology profiles columns, samples values, generates embeddings and calls NVIDIA NIM | Not described on the ontology page. Dimensions can reference a Cortex Search service (from the documentation, YAML spec) | Gap. Snowflake has no documented profiling or sampling step in the ontology |
| Natural-language query | AutoOntology query gateway returns generated SQL and rows | Cortex Analyst answers over semantic views. Only approved ontology nodes are served to Cortex (from the documentation) | Covered in design; query is in preview. Needs a synthetic check before use |
| Access control | Source reader login, SELECT on exact tables, no write grants | Per-domain READ, SUGGEST, MODIFY, OWNERSHIP (from the documentation) | Covered for domains. Table-level reader scope not documented |
| Drift detection | Sync and verify scripts re-check table identities and counts | Cortex Sense drift for bound semantic-view changes "not available yet" (from the documentation) | Gap on Snowflake's side, now |
| Bind to non-Snowflake stores (MDM and RDM in Postgres) | Imports from MDM, RDM and Silver | Bindings listed are Snowflake objects only (tables, views, Iceberg, semantic views). Postgres binding not checked | Likely gap. Would need data in Snowflake Postgres or a sync to Snowflake tables |
| Graph and reasoning | AutoOntology graph; OpenMetadata glossary graph | Bounded graph view; no SPARQL or OWL reasoning (from the documentation) | Snowflake is less capable. We do not use reasoning today |

## Verdict

Business Ontology covers the term, definition, approval and term-to-asset binding duties. It does not cover typed relations as a governed vocabulary, profiling or embeddings, or Postgres sources. It is private preview, so it is not a basis for a production decision today. Semantic views (GA for definition) cover the join and metric layer that Cortex Analyst reads. Confidence: high on status, medium on the query preview status.
