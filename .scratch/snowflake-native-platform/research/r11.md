## The reading of the operator's goal

"Removing the need for AutoOntology and OpenMetadata" does not hold today: the glossary, typed relations, the exposure manifest and profile pages have no public Snowflake equivalent, and the one candidate is private preview. The additive framing is therefore right: keep both, add Snowflake as a third target. r4 found no glossary in the public documentation and r5 found terms in the private-preview Business Ontology; both are true, they read different pages.

## Decision: no always-on pool

Operator, 2026-11-24 17:40 ET: "I also don't want always on pool".

- The design is a pool that exists only for a load: resumed by the load, auto-suspend at the shortest the service allows (60 s was accepted in the probe), nothing running between loads. The loader runs as a job service, which ends when the load ends; no long-lived service holds a node.
- The cost to price is therefore one load window, not 24 hours: credits per node-hour of the chosen family times the window's hours plus the roughly 100 s start measured in the probe. The metering rows from the probe pool give the node-hour rate after about 20:40 ET.
- Consequences to check in the follow-up: the pool's start time adds about two minutes to a load; the Snowflake Postgres instance and the monitoring Streamlit app are the only parts that stay on, and each needs its own on-demand answer (suspend or stop the instance between loads, or accept its cost) before a build.
