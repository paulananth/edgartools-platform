## The reading of the operator's goal

"Removing the need for AutoOntology and OpenMetadata" does not hold today: the glossary, typed relations, the exposure manifest and profile pages have no public Snowflake equivalent, and the one candidate is private preview. The additive framing is therefore right: keep both, add Snowflake as a third target. r4 found no glossary in the public documentation and r5 found terms in the private-preview Business Ontology; both are true, they read different pages.

## Order

1. Catalog and ontology read-only (project the same rules into semantic views and tags); no data moves.
2. The engine seam (r8's nine adapter places), so one image runs on three platforms.
3. One probe: a service reaching Snowflake Postgres **(check)**, and a timed load window to price the pool **(check)**.
4. Only then the loader as a job service.
