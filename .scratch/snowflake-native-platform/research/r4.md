# R4: Catalog duties, Horizon against OpenMetadata

## Question

Which OpenMetadata duties does Snowflake Horizon Catalog cover natively, and
which of ours does it not (glossary edges, exposure manifest, rule-driven
profile pages, quality results, lineage)? Covered, partial or missing.

Sources: Snowflake documentation only. No account touched, no provider contacted.
All claims are "from the documentation"; none is "measured".
Our duties come from `skills/openmetadata/SKILL.md` and `skills/data-catalog/SKILL.md`
(read only). The exposure-tag and Context Center references were not read.

## Preview and not found

- Preview (labelled on the cited pages, high confidence): Horizon Context
  (released 2026-10-12); metadata connectors for PostgreSQL, SQL Server,
  Tableau, Power BI and dbt.
- Not found (high confidence it is not documented as GA; medium that no preview
  glossary exists): a business glossary. Two searches and the overview found none.
  The Horizon Context release note was truncated, so its full scope is unconfirmed.
