Label: `wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A single, evidenced proposal to Codex/Grok: for the **Person** identity
kind only, allow an automatic binding rule whose measured precision is
**≥ 99%** (one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on independently labeled
held-out decisions — Clean MDM's own method) instead of the accepted Q11
**≥ 99.9%**, together with the cross-reference identifier and tier model
the Person Consumer Contract decided. Ready for them to accept, adapt, or
reject. This map does not implement anything and does not touch Clean MDM's
own files.
